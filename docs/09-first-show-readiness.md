# First show readiness plan

## Calendar and commitment

The requested dates are Wednesday October 14 for a testable build, Thursday October 15 for rehearsal, and Friday October 16 or Saturday October 17, 2026 for the weather-dependent show. Use America/New_York for this schedule. The venue and weather decision remain with the artist.

The complete described product, both native store releases, rich cinematic polish and every hardware integration cannot be responsibly promised in this window. A production web event version is a stretch target if implementation starts promptly, credentials and media are ready, a permitted provider works, and the audio setup passes. Implementation is authorized and the supplied GitHub, Render and Neon resources are recorded in `README.md`. See `PROGRESS.md` for current merged work and remaining deployment, provider and physical rehearsal gates.

Do not replace the real audience experience with a demo to meet the date. If readiness is red, keep the first show traditional and target the second or third show for the application. Continue authorized development after the event gate; the date is not an excuse to stop the broader V1 plan.

## Required first event capabilities

Real mobile participation and five-point allocation; one authoritative source-track boundary; protected core plus winning Sound Waves; a licensed real generation; durable free downloads; public display with readable Sound Waves and both QR codes; basic likes, dislikes and comments; performer statistics, private cue and Play next; contact preferences and release email for verified subscribers; clear job failures; declared recording tier; and local album playback through internet loss.

Native store apps, advanced badges, distributor automation, 1,000-person certification, advanced studio exports and the most elaborate shader effects can follow within full V1. The initial display must still express the 3D Sound Wave concept. A simple usable live leaderboard and automatic musical avatar belong in the event candidate; advanced superlatives follow.

Email, push and cloud generation need connectivity. If email delivery is not verified, remove that promise from the event invitation and record the limitation; do not say the full notification requirement is complete. A reduced recording tier can support a small first event only when labeled and chosen explicitly. A clean-microphone requirement remains open when the hardware cannot supply it.

## Thursday rehearsal script

1. Prepare the real set, core prompts, buy link and two stable QR codes. Download the entire album and test the backup playback device.
2. Verify main output, cue isolation and the recording input with spoken channel labels. Make and listen to a short recording. Confirm that the bundled microphones are actually present in the selected capture tier.
3. Join with at least one physical iPhone and one physical Android device, plus representative guests. Submit duplicate and conflicting waves; move all five votes; hide one wave; switch an account to anonymous.
4. Play through several real track boundaries. Confirm one snapshot per boundary, core preservation, accurate winning waves, visible delayed generation and a common publication state.
5. Download a released track from a phone, listen beyond half, navigate away and submit feedback. Confirm that private artist previews do not inflate play counts.
6. Receive opted-in email and available push on actual devices. Deny notification permission on another device and verify participation still works.
7. Privately preview a crowd track and select Play next close to a boundary. Confirm the intended next item changes once, and the remaining album order survives.
8. Disconnect internet for ten minutes while music and local recording continue. Reconnect, reject expired offline votes, reconcile uncertain rounds and resume without duplicate generation charges.
9. Restart API or worker in the rehearsal environment, exercise provider failure and restore a backed-up sample. Force a recording interruption and inspect recovered chunks.
10. Run a full set, record performance/capacity evidence, check outdoor screen legibility and QR scanning, and choose the supported attendance cap.

## Go or no go

Go only if the artist can finish the show using the declared hardware, recording and connectivity profile, and every critical G0–G4 condition is met. A generation delay can be a disclosed degraded experience; lost accepted votes, duplicate paid requests, cue leakage, broken downloads, unauthorized media access or unstable public playback are release blockers.

Assign one crew contact where possible to watch moderation, battery, network and health indicators. If there is no crew, simplify the artist console to Now, Next, Favorites and Continue album only. Avoid changing code during the show. Keep printed QR codes and a short URL visible even if the screen is unavailable.

At event end, close participation, finish or visibly fail outstanding jobs, release remaining validated music, finalize recordings, send only chosen event emails and capture a post-show report. Preserve raw captures before any cleanup. The archive becomes the ongoing destination for remote listeners.
